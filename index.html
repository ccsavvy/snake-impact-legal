This Privacy Policy explains how CCSavvy Studios processes information when you use Snake Impact. CCSavvy Studios is the developer, publisher, and controller responsible for the app’s data practices. Contact: snakeimpact.privacy@gmail.com.

Information processed

  • Account and profile: Firebase user ID (anonymous and/or Google-linked), display name/username, friend code, account status, personal bests, games played, and related profile timestamps.
  • Gameplay: scores, game modes (such as Classic and Maze), run results, score timestamps, and queued synchronization records for offline-first play.
  • Social: friend requests, friendships, and related identifiers needed to operate social features.
  • Online matches: private room codes, room participants, shared match configuration/seed, input events, and snapshots needed to synchronize private matches.
  • Leaderboards: world, daily, and friends ranking entries associated with your account and mode.

Snake Impact does not include an advertising SDK and does not sell personal information. It does not request contacts, precise location, camera, microphone, SMS, or payment information.
